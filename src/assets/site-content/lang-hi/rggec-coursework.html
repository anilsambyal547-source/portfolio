RGGEC Academic Coursework (2025–2029)

Undergraduate studies in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at Rajiv Gandhi Government Engineering College (RGGEC), Kangra at Nagrota Bagwan, Himachal Pradesh.

Curriculum Highlights

  • Data Structures & Algorithmic Design: Graph algorithms, dynamic programming, space-time complexity analysis.
  • Linear Algebra & Probability Theory: Matrix decompositions, vector spaces, Bayesian statistics for machine learning.
  • Object-Oriented Programming & Systems: Modular software engineering, memory management, and concurrent processes.
  • Data Mining & Exploratory Analysis: Statistical inference, multivariate regressions, feature selection.

Laboratory Projects

Hands-on implementations in Python, C++, and database architectures supporting rigorous theoretical concepts.
