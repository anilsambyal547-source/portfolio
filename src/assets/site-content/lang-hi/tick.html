Philosophy & Editorial Design

Most task management applications suffer from feature bloat, complex menus, and ambiguous state transitions.

The Daily Tasker lets you brain-dump your thoughts into a vintage, distraction-free "Newspaper" themed notepad. Once your thoughts are written, integrated Gemini AI instantly extracts, prioritizes, and categorizes actionable tasks.

Core Capabilities

  • Intelligent Extraction: Uses Google Gemini 2.5 Flash via React Server Actions to securely parse unstructured notes into DO, LEARN, and PROOF task categories.
  • Dependency Resolution: AI understands chronological sequencing (e.g., "do X after Y") and orders your Agenda into a logical Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG).
  • Simple Mode: Full-screen distraction-free editor with built-in auto-saving manual TO-DO list.
  • Zero SSR Mismatches: Client-side hydration safely syncs work to localStorage with zero-latency instant caching.
  • Modern Stack: Built with Next.js 14 App Router, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. Deployed live on Vercel.
