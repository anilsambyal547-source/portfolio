Overview & Problem Statement

Rural health centers and primary clinics frequently face connectivity constraints, scarce medical staffing, and convoluted patient record systems.

Rural Care was engineered to streamline patient triage, organize preliminary assessments, and provide clear decision-support tools for community healthcare workers.

Key Features

  • Offline-First Resilience: Built to function reliably in areas with low or intermittent network bandwidth.
  • Intelligent Triage Assistance: Structured workflows to help health workers prioritize urgent cases.
  • Patient Case Summaries: Automated synthesis of vital metrics and patient history for visiting physicians.
  • Accessible Interface: Clean, high-contrast, distraction-free UI built with TypeScript and modern Next.js.

Technical Architecture

Constructed with TypeScript, Next.js, and modular UI components. The system separates sensitive records from triage compute, ensuring privacy compliance and rapid local performance.

Hackathon Journey

Developed with teammates in an intensive hackathon environment, translating real-world healthcare disparities into a working software product.
