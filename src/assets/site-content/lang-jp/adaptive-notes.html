Overview

Standard summarization tools often produce generic, shallow outputs that lose subtle technical formulas, definitions, or hierarchical context.

The Adaptive Note Summarizer transforms complex documents into clear, actionable insights through semantic density evaluation, vector retrieval, and multi-model intelligence.

Core Capabilities

  • Multi-Model Intelligence: Dynamically discovers and uses Google Gemini models (including Gemini 3.1 and Gemini 2.x).
  • Advanced OCR & Vision: Extracts text from images (PNG, JPG, TIFF, WEBP) and scanned PDFs seamlessly.
  • RAG Engine: Powered by FAISS and Sentence Transformers to answer questions by retrieving verified, attributed document segments.
  • Smart Semantic Chunking: Breaks large technical documents into meaningful conceptual units for high-fidelity synthesis.
  • Multi-Format Ingestion: Ingests PDFs (text & scanned), DOCX, TXT, and lecture notes.
  • Modern UI: Glassmorphic dark interface with real-time processing indicators and interactive Q&A.

Why I Built This

As an AI and Data Science student handling rigorous coursework in algorithms, machine learning, and statistics, having dense study material condensed without losing mathematical rigor is indispensable.
