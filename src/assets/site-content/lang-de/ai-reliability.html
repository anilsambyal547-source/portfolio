AI Reliability & Systems Dependability

Research notes, experiments, and architectural patterns on engineering dependable AI systems.

The Reliability Dilemma

Machine learning models and Large Language Models are inherently probabilistic. In real-world applications — such as clinical triage or academic research — hallucinations and silent failures are unacceptable.

Core Research Pillars

  • Deterministic Guardrails: Constraining output spaces using structured schemas and AST validation.
  • Ground-Truth Verification: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines that enforce strict attribution to source citations.
  • Evaluation Metrics: Moving beyond token loss toward semantic consistency, boundary stress testing, and adversarial benchmarking.
