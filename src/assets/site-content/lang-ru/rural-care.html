Overview & Problem Statement

Rural communities face delayed diagnosis, limited specialist access, long travel times for consultations, and unreliable internet connectivity.

GraminCare bridges these disparities by connecting ASHA workers, patients, primary health centers, and doctors into a unified digital ecosystem that continues functioning during complete internet outages.

Complete Clinical Workflow

ASHA Worker → Patient Registration → AI Triage → Doctor Consultation → Prescription → Follow-up

  • AI Health Assistant: Multilingual voice and text-based symptom triage and early warning alerts.
  • Offline-First Resilience: Built with local caching and offline queues so community health workers never lose patient logs during connectivity drops.
  • Doctor Teleconsultation: Low-bandwidth audio and video consultations optimized for rural mobile networks.
  • One-Click Emergency SOS: Real-time distress alert with GPS location and patient vital history.
  • Digital Prescriptions & Health Records: Centralized patient records, ABHA-ready integration, and QR-based medicine inventory tracking.

Technical Stack

Engineered with Next.js, TypeScript strict mode, Tailwind CSS, offline storage synchronization, and modular UI components for responsive mobile and tablet usage.
